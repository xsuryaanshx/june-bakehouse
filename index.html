The Space

A room that earns a second visit.

The moment you step through the arched doorway at 12 Patrakar Colony, something slows down. Double-height ceilings, rattan pendants, upholstered oak chairs and concrete floors — it's the kind of place that took its time to get right, and you can feel it.

The kitchen and the counter are the heart of it: a glass pastry case, the hiss of an espresso machine, and a team that actually knows what's in everything. Coffee comes out calibrated, not guessed at. Pastries rotate with the season — the Christmas edition involved dried citrus, gilded baskets and exactly zero kitschy Santas.

Dine-in or takeaway. Either way, there's no rush.

What We Serve

From the first coffee to the last slice.

A short list of what people keep coming back for. Full menu available at the counter — or ask what's just come out of the oven.

Coffee

  • Cortado Equal parts espresso and warm milk. Short, precise, nothing extra.
  • Latte — with a side cookie Silky, well-pulled. The cookie is not optional.
  • Matcha Ceremonial-grade, served hot or iced.
  • Filter & Pour-over Manual brews, rotated by origin.

Bakery & Desserts

  • Pistachio Berliner A generously filled German-style doughnut. The one everyone mentions.
  • Nutella Cheesecake Dense, not cloying. Finishes clean.
  • Carrot Cake Spiced, with a proper cream cheese frosting.
  • French Cake Slices Rotating selection — ask what's in the case today.

Pizza & Pasta

  • Neapolitan Pizza Thin base, high-temperature bake, toppings that don't crowd each other.
  • Pasta Italian-style. Regulars order it more than they'll admit.

Toasts & Toasties

  • Mushroom Open Toast One of those things you didn't expect to love.
  • Custom Toasties Built to order. The grilled bread alone is worth it.

Full menu at the counter. Prices and availability may vary — some things sell out early.
